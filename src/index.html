About

I started where most infrastructure people start: in the server room. Windows Server, VMware, backups, and the laptops on every desk. Six years and three countries later, I spend my days on Azure, Terraform and identity, but the job hasn't really changed. Keep the platform steady, so the people on top of it can move.

Most of that time has been in business-critical, regulated environments: global pharma, logistics, and now a fast-moving engineering company with its own software. Places where a bad change is expensive. That taught me to test first, write things down, and ask before assuming.

I'm Portuguese, based in Utrecht. Outside work you'll find me doing sports, or building something small for the web, which is how this site happened.

How I work

  1. 01

    Test first

    In environments where a bad change is expensive, I'd rather spend ten minutes proving it in dev than an hour explaining it in production.

  2. 02

    Write it down

    Runbooks, decision records and pull requests that explain why. The next person, often me, shouldn't have to guess.

  3. 03

    Ask before assuming

    I don't know everything about cloud and I'm fine saying so. A short question to a colleague beats an hour of guessing.

  4. 04

    Finish what I pick up

    Ownership doesn't end at "deployed". It includes the alert afterwards, and the fix that stops it coming back.
