About

I started in the server room: Windows Server, VMware, backups, and the laptop on every desk. Six years and three countries later, my days are Azure, Terraform and identity. The tools changed. The job didn't: keep the platform steady, so the people building on it can move fast.

Most of that time was in business-critical, regulated environments: global pharma, logistics, and now an engineering company that ships its own software. Places where a bad change is expensive. They taught me to test first, write things down, and ask before assuming.

I'm Portuguese and live in Utrecht. Away from work I'm usually doing sports, or building something small for the web. This site is one of those.

How I work

  1. 01

    Test first

    When a bad change is expensive, ten minutes proving it in dev beats an hour explaining it in production.

  2. 02

    Write it down

    Runbooks, decision records, pull requests that explain why. The next person, often me, shouldn't have to guess.

  3. 03

    Ask before assuming

    I don't know everything about cloud, and I'll say so. A two-minute question beats an hour of guessing.

  4. 04

    Finish what I pick up

    Deployed isn't done. Done includes the alert afterwards, and the fix that stops it coming back.
